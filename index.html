USB and Bluetooth need Chrome or Edge. Flash the sketch once from the Arduino IDE. Bluetooth can be joined by anyone nearby, so prefer USB.

Parents see focused and distracted minutes, notifications, pictures and a live view while a session runs. A badge shows on this screen whenever a parent is watching live.
